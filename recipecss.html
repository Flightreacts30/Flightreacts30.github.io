Crispy Chicken Tender Recipe

Our story starts in a Manchester, New Hampshire restaurant that had been started by two Greek immigrants in 1917...

More History About Chicken Tenders

"These crunchy baked chicken tenders are life changing! The secret to truly golden, truly crunchy baked breaded chicken is to toast the breadcrumbs in the oven first..."

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 1/2 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  • 500 g chicken tenderloins

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C/390°F.
  2. Toast panko until golden.
  3. Place a rack on a baking tray.
  4. Mix batter ingredients and coat chicken.
